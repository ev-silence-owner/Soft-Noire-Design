# Non-clinical

This is not a medical device. Not therapy. Not diagnosis. Not an attention score.

Forbidden in consumer copy, contracts, UI, and logs shown to the user:

- diagnosis, therapy, treatment, cure
- medical device language
- HRV, biomarkers, “this calms you”
- “scientifically proven golden ratio”
- wellbeing / concentration / outcome claims without a comparative study
- gamification of silence (points, streaks as moral worth, leaderboards of stillness)
- LIVE claims without HTTPS evidence
- countdown that creates pressure
- score during Pulse Tap or Golden Silence

Pulse metrics (median ITI, CV, phase-lock) may be stored locally for adaptation. They are not shown as a grade.

Boundary badge: **NON-CLINICAL**.
